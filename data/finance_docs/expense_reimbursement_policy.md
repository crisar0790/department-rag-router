
# Employee Expense Reimbursement Policy

Document ID: FIN-001
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

AR Cloud reimburses employees for reasonable, necessary, and properly authorized expenses incurred while performing legitimate company activities.

This policy establishes the requirements for submitting, reviewing, approving, and processing employee expense reimbursement requests. It is intended to maintain financial accountability, ensure consistent treatment of employees, and protect company resources.

The policy applies to employees who incur business expenses related to approved travel, client meetings, professional activities, office requirements, and other authorized operational needs.

Reimbursement is not automatic simply because an employee paid for an item or service while working. Expenses must have a clear business purpose and comply with applicable authorization requirements.

Finance administers the reimbursement process, verifies supporting documentation, and coordinates approved payments. Department managers are responsible for confirming the business necessity of expenses submitted by their teams.

## Eligible business expenses

An eligible expense must be directly connected to an authorized business activity and must be reasonable in relation to its purpose.

Common eligible expenses include transportation for approved business travel, accommodation during authorized work assignments, meals associated with approved travel, and necessary purchases made on behalf of the company.

Professional registration fees, training materials, and business event expenses may qualify when approved by the responsible manager.

Employees may also request reimbursement for emergency operational purchases when delaying the purchase would significantly affect an authorized business activity.

Expenses related to personal preferences, convenience, or activities unrelated to company operations are not eligible.

Finance may request additional information when the business purpose of an expense is unclear.

An expense category being generally eligible does not remove the requirement for appropriate authorization.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

AR Cloud does not reimburse personal purchases or costs unrelated to approved company activities.

Examples include personal entertainment, leisure activities, personal subscriptions, non-business gifts, and expenses incurred by accompanying family members.

Fines, penalties, and costs resulting from violations of applicable rules are generally not reimbursable.

Additional charges caused by avoidable personal decisions, such as unnecessary accommodation upgrades or optional premium services, may be rejected.

Employees must not submit the same expense more than once or request reimbursement for amounts already paid directly by AR Cloud.

Expenses that lack sufficient supporting documentation may be rejected unless an authorized exception applies.

Finance evaluates unusual situations individually and may consult the responsible department manager before making a final determination.

## Prior authorization requirements

Employees must obtain approval before incurring expenses that require prior authorization.

Business travel, significant equipment purchases, external training, and other planned expenditures must be reviewed by the responsible manager before financial commitments are made.

Authorization should identify the business purpose, expected cost, and relevant operational requirements.

Approval of an activity does not automatically authorize unlimited spending.

When the estimated cost changes significantly, the employee must request additional approval before proceeding whenever practical.

Emergency expenses may be reviewed after purchase when obtaining prior authorization was not reasonably possible.

In these situations, the employee must explain the circumstances and provide supporting evidence.

Retroactive approval is an exception and does not guarantee reimbursement.

## Expense documentation requirements

Every reimbursement request must include sufficient documentation to verify the expense.

Acceptable supporting documents may include invoices, receipts, electronic payment confirmations, or other records approved by Finance.

Documentation should identify the vendor, transaction date, amount paid, currency, and purchased goods or services.

Employees must provide a clear explanation of the business purpose and identify the related activity or project when applicable.

When a receipt contains both business and personal purchases, the employee must identify the eligible portion.

Altered, misleading, or intentionally incomplete documentation is not acceptable.

Finance may request additional evidence when the original documentation does not establish the legitimacy or amount of the expense.

Employees should retain original supporting records until the reimbursement process is completed.

## Submission deadlines

Employees should submit reimbursement requests within 15 calendar days after the expense is incurred or the associated business trip is completed.

Submitting expenses promptly allows Finance to maintain accurate financial records and process payments efficiently.

Requests submitted after the standard deadline must include an explanation for the delay.

Late submission does not automatically result in rejection, but Finance may require additional review or authorization.

Employees must not intentionally delay submissions to transfer expenses into a different accounting period.

When an expense occurs near the end of a financial reporting period, Finance may request earlier submission to support accounting close activities.

Department managers should remind employees to submit outstanding expenses before extended absences or the completion of major projects.

## Reimbursement request preparation

Employees must prepare reimbursement requests using the company's approved submission process.

Each request should identify the employee, department, expense date, category, amount, currency, and business justification.

Supporting documentation must be attached or made available through the approved internal system.

When multiple expenses relate to the same trip or activity, employees should organize them clearly to simplify review.

The employee is responsible for checking that the submitted information matches the supporting documents.

Requests containing incorrect amounts, missing receipts, or unclear descriptions may be returned for correction.

Employees must not divide a single purchase into multiple reimbursement requests to avoid approval requirements.

## Manager review and approval

The responsible department manager reviews the business justification of submitted expenses.

Manager approval confirms that the expense was related to authorized work and was appropriate for the activity performed.

Managers must verify that the expense was reasonably necessary and consistent with any previously approved budget.

Approval must not be granted solely because the employee has already paid the expense.

When an expense exceeds an authorized amount, the manager must explain whether the additional cost was justified.

Managers may reject expenses that are unnecessary, excessive, or unrelated to company activities.

Finance retains responsibility for validating financial documentation and policy compliance even when manager approval has been granted.

## Finance verification process

Finance reviews reimbursement requests after the required managerial authorization has been obtained.

The verification process includes checking expense categories, supporting documents, amounts, dates, and potential duplicate submissions.

Finance may compare reimbursement requests with company payment records to determine whether an expense has already been paid.

Requests involving unusual amounts or inconsistent documentation may require additional investigation.

Finance may return a request for clarification without formally rejecting it.

If the request meets the applicable requirements, Finance authorizes it for payment processing.

A request must not be marked as completed until the appropriate financial processing activities have been recorded.

## Payment processing and timing

Approved reimbursements are processed through AR Cloud's established payment procedures.

Finance aims to process complete and approved reimbursement requests within 10 business days after final verification.

This period is an operational target rather than an unconditional payment guarantee.

Processing may take longer when documentation is incomplete, approval is pending, banking information requires verification, or exceptional circumstances affect financial operations.

Employees must provide accurate payment information through approved company channels.

Finance must not request sensitive banking credentials, passwords, or authentication codes as part of reimbursement processing.

Employees may contact Finance to request information about the status of a submitted reimbursement.

## Business travel expenses

Employees traveling for approved business purposes must follow the applicable travel authorization requirements.

Transportation, accommodation, and meals may be reimbursable when they are necessary and reasonable for the approved assignment.

Employees should select cost-effective options that meet legitimate operational and safety requirements.

Personal travel extensions, leisure activities, and unrelated purchases must be separated from business expenses.

When a trip includes both personal and business activities, only the authorized business portion is eligible for reimbursement.

Employees must document unusual circumstances that increase travel costs, such as unexpected cancellations or required schedule changes.

Finance may request travel itineraries or other supporting information when necessary to validate a claim.

## Meals and hospitality expenses

Meals may qualify for reimbursement when they are directly associated with approved travel, client meetings, or authorized business activities.

Routine personal meals during normal workdays are not automatically reimbursable.

Requests involving client hospitality must identify the business purpose and relevant participants.

Employees should avoid excessive spending and select options appropriate to the professional context.

Alcoholic beverages and entertainment expenses require explicit authorization when reimbursement is permitted under applicable internal rules.

Finance may reject hospitality expenses that lack a legitimate business justification.

Employees must not describe personal gatherings as business meetings to obtain reimbursement.

## Foreign currency expenses

Expenses incurred in foreign currencies must be reported using the original transaction currency and amount.

Employees must provide supporting documentation showing the actual expense.

Finance determines the applicable conversion method according to the company's accounting procedures.

When a payment card statement identifies the converted amount charged to the employee, Finance may use that information when appropriate.

Employees must not select arbitrary exchange rates to increase the reimbursement amount.

Foreign transaction fees may be considered when they are directly associated with an approved business expense.

Any exchange rate adjustment must be documented sufficiently to support financial review.

## Lost receipts and exceptional circumstances

Employees who cannot provide a required receipt must notify Finance and explain the circumstances.

The employee should provide alternative evidence when available, such as payment confirmations or transaction records.

A missing receipt declaration does not automatically establish that an expense is eligible.

Finance evaluates the business purpose, amount, available evidence, and applicable approval requirements.

Exceptional reimbursements may require authorization from an appropriate manager or designated financial authority.

Employees must not create replacement receipts or alter existing documents to support a claim.

Repeated missing documentation may result in additional review or corrective guidance.

## Rejected requests and corrections

Finance may reject reimbursement requests that do not satisfy the policy requirements.

Common reasons include missing documentation, insufficient business justification, duplicate submissions, unauthorized spending, and non-reimbursable expense categories.

Employees must receive an explanation identifying the reason for rejection or the information required for correction.

When a request is incomplete rather than ineligible, Finance may return it for revision.

Employees may submit corrected information through the approved process.

Disagreements concerning eligibility should be reviewed by Finance and the responsible manager.

A rejected request must not be resubmitted with misleading information or an altered description of the original expense.

## Record retention and confidentiality

Finance maintains reimbursement records to support accounting accuracy, internal controls, and applicable retention requirements.

Records may include expense reports, supporting receipts, approval decisions, payment confirmations, and related communications.

Access must be limited to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

Employee financial information must not be shared through unauthorized communication channels.

Finance must store reimbursement records in approved company systems.

Employees and managers must not retain unnecessary copies of sensitive reimbursement documentation in personal storage services.

Retention and deletion activities must follow applicable organizational requirements.

## Roles and responsibilities

Employees are responsible for obtaining required authorization, spending company resources appropriately, and submitting accurate reimbursement requests.

Department managers confirm business necessity, review budget implications, and approve eligible expenses within their authority.

Finance verifies documentation, checks compliance, maintains financial records, and coordinates reimbursement payments.

Technology supports the security and availability of approved systems used to submit and process reimbursement requests.

Employees must report suspected duplicate payments, incorrect reimbursements, or other financial irregularities.

All participants must maintain appropriate confidentiality and avoid conflicts of interest during the reimbursement process.

## Policy review and exceptions

Finance periodically reviews this policy to ensure that reimbursement procedures remain consistent with AR Cloud's operational requirements.

Reviews may consider recurring submission problems, processing delays, employee feedback, and changes in accounting practices.

Exceptions must be documented and approved by the appropriate financial authority.

An exception granted for one expense does not automatically establish a general entitlement for future claims.

Finance communicates significant policy changes to affected employees and department managers.

This policy complements the company's financial approval procedures, information security requirements, and applicable business travel arrangements.
