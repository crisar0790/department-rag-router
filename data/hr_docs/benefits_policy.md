# Employee Benefits Policy

Document ID: HR-005
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This policy describes the benefits available to eligible full-time
AR Cloud employees. Benefits are separate from salary, annual
vacation, and leave entitlements.

Contractors are not covered by this policy unless their individual
agreement explicitly includes a benefit.

This document contains fictional company rules for an educational
project. It does not establish legal entitlements or describe
benefits offered by a real employer.

## Eligibility and activation

Full-time employees become eligible for the benefits described
here on their confirmed employment start date, unless a specific
benefit states a different condition.

HR verifies eligibility and records benefit enrollment in the
employee portal. Employees must review their enrollment details
and report missing or incorrect information to HR.

Eligibility does not mean that every benefit activates
automatically. Employees must complete the enrollment or request
procedure associated with each benefit.

This document cannot confirm an individual's enrollment status.
Employees must consult their portal record or contact HR.

## Wellness allowance

Eligible employees may claim up to USD 300 per calendar year
for approved wellness activities, including gym memberships,
fitness classes, and guided meditation subscriptions.

The allowance does not cover medical treatment, medication,
equipment purchases, travel, or subscriptions intended primarily
for entertainment.

Employees must request HR approval through the portal before
purchasing an activity they intend to claim. The request must
identify the provider, activity, dates, and expected cost.

After approval and purchase, employees submit the receipt through
the Finance expense workflow, referencing the HR approval.
Finance verifies the payment documentation.

An HR approval confirms benefit eligibility. It does not confirm
that a payment has been processed.

## Wellness allowance limits

The annual USD 300 limit applies to the total approved claims
for that calendar year. Unused amounts expire at year-end and
cannot be carried over or exchanged for cash.

An approved purchase above the remaining allowance is reimbursed
only up to the available amount. The employee is responsible
for the difference.

Employees joining during the year receive the full annual limit
under this fictional policy. HR handles disputes about the
recorded allowance balance.

This document does not specify reimbursement timing or currency
conversion rules. Those questions follow the Finance procedure.

## Professional development allowance

Eligible employees may request up to USD 600 per calendar year
for role-related courses, certifications, or professional events.

The direct manager must confirm that the activity supports the
employee's current responsibilities or agreed development plan.
HR then confirms allowance eligibility and available budget.

Requests must be submitted at least 10 working days before
registration. Employees must receive both confirmations before
making a purchase intended for reimbursement.

Approval is not guaranteed. Activities unrelated to the employee's
role or development plan are outside this benefit.

Purchases of personal computers and general office equipment
are not covered by the professional development allowance.

## Learning expenses and working time

After an approved learning purchase, employees submit the receipt
through the Finance expense workflow with the manager and HR
approval references.

The annual allowance does not carry over into the next year.
An approved activity costing more than the available allowance
requires the employee to pay the difference unless a separate
budget exception has been confirmed.

Financial approval does not automatically approve study time
during working hours. Employees must agree attendance and study
arrangements with their manager before the activity begins.

The learning allowance does not automatically create additional
paid leave or alter annual vacation entitlement.

## Employee assistance service

Eligible employees may access six confidential support sessions
per calendar year through the employee assistance provider.

Employees obtain the provider's booking instructions from the
benefits section of the portal. HR can explain eligibility and
help locate those instructions but does not book sessions on
the employee's behalf.

The company does not receive the content of individual sessions.
Employees must not upload session notes or sensitive personal
information to a general support ticket.

The service is not an emergency response channel. This document
does not provide medical advice or determine the suitability
of a particular service for an individual.

## Corrections and benefit changes

Employees must report enrollment errors or incorrect allowance
balances to HR through the portal.

HR reviews the record and confirms any correction. Employees
should provide the relevant request or approval reference without
including unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes to the benefits described in this policy require an
updated policy version and written communication from HR.
Managers cannot independently increase an annual benefit limit.

## Support boundaries

HR handles benefit eligibility, enrollment, allowance balances,
and policy interpretation. Managers assess the role relevance
of professional development activities and approve work schedules.

Finance handles receipt requirements, reimbursement processing,
currency conversion, and payment status. Technology Support
handles technical failures accessing the employee portal.

The support assistant may explain documented benefits and identify
the responsible department. It cannot inspect personal balances,
approve a purchase, book confidential sessions, or process
reimbursement.
