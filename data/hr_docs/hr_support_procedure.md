# HR Support and Escalation Procedure

Document ID: HR-007
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how full-time AR Cloud employees contact
HR, track administrative requests, and escalate unresolved matters.

It applies to questions about HR policies, employee records,
benefits eligibility, onboarding coordination, and leave procedures.

The procedure is fictional and intended for an educational project.
It does not replace an emergency service or establish legal
complaint-handling requirements.

## Standard support channel

Employees submit routine HR questions through the employee portal's
HR support section.

Each request should include a clear subject, a description of
the question, the relevant policy or request reference when
available, and any date that affects the request.

Employees should avoid including personal information that is
not necessary to understand the issue.

Medical documents, identity documents, and bank details must use
the designated secure sections described in the relevant procedure.
They must not be attached to an ordinary support request.

## Availability and initial response

HR support operates Monday through Friday, from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC,
excluding company holidays.

Routine requests have an initial response target of two working
days. A request received outside support hours is considered
received at the next opening of the support service.

The initial response may acknowledge the request, answer the
question, or ask for additional information.

This target does not guarantee resolution within two working
days. Completion depends on the request, required verification,
and involvement of other departments.

## Procedure-specific timelines

Some HR procedures define their own review or processing targets.
Those targets apply to the corresponding action.

For example, the Personal Information Update Procedure, document
HR-006, defines a three-working-day initial review target for
record changes. The general two-working-day support target does
not replace that procedure's review target.

A support acknowledgment does not mean that a requested record
change, benefit approval, or leave decision has been completed.

Employees should consult the relevant policy when asking about
an action deadline.

## Time-sensitive requests

Employees should mark a request as time-sensitive when a confirmed
deadline is within the next two working days.

The request must explain the deadline and the impact of a delayed
response. HR reviews the priority based on those details.

A time-sensitive label does not guarantee immediate handling,
approval, or an exception to a policy.

Urgent absence notifications must follow the Leave Policy,
document HR-002. Employees must not wait for a routine support
response before notifying their manager about an unexpected absence.

## Request tracking and additional information

The portal assigns a reference to each request. Employees should
use that reference when providing additional information or
asking for an update.

If HR requests clarification, employees should reply in the same
request rather than open another one.

When necessary information is missing, HR may place the request
in a waiting-for-employee status. The recorded status must explain
what information is required.

Requests waiting for employee information are not treated as
completed. Once the information is received, HR communicates
the next action or an updated estimate.

## First escalation

If a routine request has not received an initial response within
two working days, the employee should add a follow-up to the
existing request.

The follow-up should identify the original submission date,
request reference, and any relevant deadline.

If there is still no response after one additional working day,
the employee may request escalation to the HR coordinator through
the same support channel.

For procedure-specific actions, employees must follow that
procedure's deadline before reporting a missed review target.

## Unresolved or disputed decisions

If an employee disagrees with an HR response, they should explain
which part of the response is disputed and provide the relevant
policy or supporting facts.

The HR coordinator reviews the matter and identifies whether
another reviewer is needed. The employee receives an initial
review acknowledgment within three working days.

An escalation does not automatically reverse a previous decision.
A policy exception requires explicit confirmation from the
authorized owner.

This document does not guarantee a fixed resolution time for
every disputed decision.

## Confidential concerns

Employees reporting a sensitive workplace concern should request
access to HR's confidential reporting channel through the portal.

They should not describe sensitive allegations in public channels
or ordinary team discussions.

If the concern involves the employee's direct manager, the employee
may contact HR without routing the concern through that manager.

If the concern involves the assigned HR reviewer, the employee
may request an alternative reviewer through the confidential
channel. HR confirms the reassignment process.

The support assistant must not investigate allegations or promise
absolute confidentiality. It may explain how to reach the
designated channel.

## Department boundaries

HR handles policy interpretation, administrative employee records,
benefit eligibility, and HR request coordination.

Technology Support handles portal access failures, authentication,
devices, and technical incidents.

Finance handles reimbursement processing, payment status, billing,
and payment-related documentation.

If a request reaches the wrong department, the receiving team
identifies the responsible team and communicates the transfer.
A transfer does not mean that the issue has been resolved.

## Closure and follow-up

HR closes a request after providing the requested information
or confirming the completed action.

If the same issue remains unresolved, the employee may request
reopening within five working days of closure.

A separate issue should be submitted as a new request with
a reference to the earlier case when relevant.

The support assistant may explain request tracking and escalation.
It cannot inspect request status, assign reviewers, reopen cases,
approve exceptions, or confirm completed HR actions.
