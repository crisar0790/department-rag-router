# Internal Mobility and Role Change Procedure

Document ID: HR-012
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how full-time AR Cloud employees apply
for internal vacancies and how approved role changes are
coordinated.

It covers eligibility review, applications, selection, transition
planning, and updates to employee records.

Contractors are outside this procedure and must contact their
contract owner about changes to their responsibilities.

This fictional procedure does not guarantee promotion, continued
employment, or a compensation increase.

## Published vacancies

HR publishes internal vacancies in the employee portal.
Each posting identifies the role, team, responsibilities,
required skills, application deadline, and selection contact.

Employees must use the published posting to confirm whether
a vacancy is open. An informal conversation about a possible
role does not establish an available position.

If a posting contains conflicting requirements or dates,
employees should contact HR before submitting an application.
HR confirms the current version of the posting.

## Eligibility

Employees may apply after completing six calendar months of
employment, unless the vacancy explicitly permits earlier
applications.

Employees must meet the required criteria in the posting.
Preferred criteria are considered during selection but are
not automatically treated as mandatory requirements.

A performance review outcome does not by itself establish
eligibility or guarantee selection.

Employees who do not meet the six-month requirement may ask HR
whether an exception is available. An exception requires written
confirmation from HR and the hiring manager before the
application is treated as eligible.

## Application procedure

Employees submit applications through the vacancy page in the
employee portal before the published deadline.

The application must include a current professional profile,
a summary of relevant experience, and a brief explanation of
interest in the role.

Employees should describe their contributions without including
confidential customer data or another employee's personal
information.

Submitting an application does not change the employee's
current role, manager, working schedule, or permissions.

## Current manager notification

Employees do not need their current manager's permission to
submit an eligible internal application.

HR informs the employee before contacting the current manager
for role-related information. Employees may discuss the
application with their manager earlier if they choose.

The current manager may provide documented information relevant
to the selection process but cannot independently cancel an
eligible application.

Concerns about inappropriate pressure or interference should
be raised through the confidential HR channel described in
the HR Support and Escalation Procedure, document HR-007.

## Selection process

The hiring manager evaluates applications against the published
role criteria and coordinates interviews with HR.

Depending on the vacancy, the process may include a role-related
exercise. The posting or interview invitation must explain
the exercise requirements and submission date.

HR normally acknowledges an application within three working
days. This acknowledgment does not confirm that the employee
has been shortlisted.

The procedure does not establish a universal selection deadline.
Employees may request an update from HR using the vacancy and
application references.

## Selection decisions and feedback

HR communicates the selection decision directly to the applicant.
An informal statement from a team member is not a confirmed offer.

Employees who are not selected may request feedback within
five working days of receiving the decision.

HR coordinates feedback with the hiring manager. Feedback
describes relevant selection criteria but does not disclose
other applicants' personal information or assessments.

A feedback request does not automatically reopen the selection
process or guarantee a different decision.

## Role change confirmation

A selected employee receives a written role change proposal
from HR. It identifies the new role, reporting manager,
effective date, and any confirmed changes to employment terms.

Employees must review the proposal and raise questions with HR
before accepting it.

A role change does not automatically increase compensation.
Any compensation change must be explicitly confirmed through
the authorized HR process.

Finance may process a confirmed payment change but does not
decide the employee's new role or compensation terms.

## Transition planning

After acceptance, the current and new managers agree a transition
plan with the employee and HR.

The plan identifies the effective date, handover tasks,
outstanding responsibilities, and any required training.

The standard planning target is five working days after acceptance.
This target does not impose a universal transition duration.

Employees remain responsible for their current assignment until
the confirmed transition arrangement takes effect.

The onboarding reviews described in document HR-003 apply to
new employees. An internal transfer does not automatically
restart new-employee onboarding or change existing benefit
eligibility.

## Technical access changes

The new manager submits required access requests to Technology
Support before the effective date.

Each request must identify the application, requested permissions,
business reason, and relevant approver.

Technology Support coordinates new access and removal of
permissions no longer required. A role change confirmation
does not automatically authorize every requested permission.

Employees must not share accounts or use another employee's
credentials while waiting for approved access.

## Records and support boundaries

HR updates the employee's role and reporting information after
the change is confirmed. Employees should report incorrect
records to HR with the role change reference.

Managers handle role expectations, handover, and operational
planning. Technology Support handles permissions and technical
provisioning. Finance handles confirmed payment processing.

The support assistant may explain application requirements
and transition responsibilities. It cannot inspect applications,
select candidates, approve exceptions, negotiate compensation,
or grant system access.
