# Leave Policy

Document ID: HR-002
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This policy describes medical, personal, and bereavement leave
for full-time AR Cloud employees. Vacation requests follow the
separate Vacation Policy, document HR-001.

Contractors follow their individual agreements and must contact
their contract owner about absence arrangements.

These rules describe a fictional company policy for an educational
project. They do not represent employment law. Parental leave,
statutory leave, and accommodations are outside this document's
scope and require consultation with HR.

## Medical leave notification

Employees unable to work because of illness must notify their
direct manager before their scheduled start time whenever possible.
If an emergency prevents notification, they should contact the
manager as soon as reasonably possible.

The notification should state that the employee cannot work and
provide an estimated return date if known. Employees do not need
to disclose their diagnosis to the manager.

The employee must record the absence in the employee portal within
two working days of the initial notification. If the employee
cannot access the portal, HR can assist with recording the absence.
Technical access problems should also be reported to Technology
Support.

## Medical documentation

Medical absences lasting three or more consecutive working days
require a certificate confirming the period during which the
employee cannot work.

The certificate must be submitted through the portal's confidential
HR upload section. Employees must not send medical documents to
team mailing lists, public channels, or handover documents.

HR reviews the certificate and contacts the employee if additional
administrative information is required. Managers receive only
the information needed to organize staffing and the expected
absence period.

For an absence shorter than three consecutive working days,
a certificate is not required under this policy.

## Medical leave recording

Medical leave is recorded separately from annual vacation.
Managers must not automatically deduct vacation days because
an employee reports an illness.

This document does not establish a universal medical leave
entitlement or payment amount. HR confirms the applicable
conditions under the employee's agreement and relevant rules.

If illness occurs during approved vacation, the employee must
notify HR and provide documentation covering the affected dates.
HR determines whether the recorded absence can be reclassified.
Vacation days are not restored automatically when a certificate
is uploaded.

## Personal leave

Full-time employees may request up to two paid working days of
personal leave per calendar year for urgent personal matters.

Personal leave does not reduce the annual vacation balance.
Unused personal leave cannot be carried over into the next year
and cannot be exchanged for payment.

Requests should be submitted through the employee portal at least
two working days in advance when the circumstances are foreseeable.
For unexpected situations, employees should notify their manager
as soon as possible and record the request afterward.

Personal leave requires manager approval. Employees needing more
than two days should contact HR to discuss another applicable
leave category or an unpaid leave arrangement.

## Bereavement leave

Full-time employees may request up to five paid working days
following the death of a spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling.

For the death of a grandparent or parent-in-law, employees may
request up to two paid working days.

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably
possible. HR records and confirms the applicable leave period.
The advance notice requirements for vacation do not apply to
bereavement leave.

Employees needing additional time for travel or family arrangements
must contact HR. Additional days are not automatically approved
and must be recorded under the arrangement confirmed by HR.

## Return and extensions

Employees should notify their manager when their expected return
date changes. Extensions must be recorded in the portal, and
medical documentation must cover any additional period for which
a certificate is required.

If an employee returns earlier than recorded, HR must update the
absence dates. Employees must not work during a period in which
they are medically certified as unable to work without first
consulting HR about the required return process.

## Support and escalation

Managers coordinate staffing and handovers. HR handles leave
classification, confidential documentation, entitlement questions,
and corrections to absence records.

If a manager is unavailable, employees should contact HR rather
than delay an urgent absence notification.

The support assistant may explain these procedures, but it cannot
approve leave, inspect personal medical documents, determine an
individual entitlement, or update employee records.
