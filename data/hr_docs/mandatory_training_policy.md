# Mandatory Training Policy

Document ID: HR-009
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This policy describes mandatory training assignments, completion
requirements, extensions, and support responsibilities for
full-time AR Cloud employees.

Mandatory training is separate from optional professional
development funded through the Employee Benefits Policy,
document HR-005.

Contractors receive training requirements from their contract
owner. This fictional policy does not establish legal training
obligations or professional certification requirements.

## Training responsibilities

HR coordinates assignments and monitors completion records.
The training owner defines course content, assessment requirements,
and acceptable completion evidence.

The direct manager ensures that employees have time within their
approved working hours to complete required activities.

Employees must review assignments, complete the activities,
and report blockers before their deadlines.

Technology Support handles technical access problems. It does
not approve training extensions or mark assignments as complete.

## Initial security awareness training

New employees must complete security awareness training within
five working days of their confirmed start date, consistent
with the Employee Onboarding Procedure, document HR-003.

The course covers account security, multi-factor authentication,
safe handling of company information, and incident reporting.

Completion requires reviewing all assigned modules and passing
the assessment with a score of at least 80 percent.

Opening the course or attending an introduction does not satisfy
the completion requirement.

## Initial policy acknowledgments

During their first five working days, employees must review and
acknowledge the Vacation Policy, document HR-001, and the Leave
Policy, document HR-002, in the employee portal.

These acknowledgments are separate checklist items from security
awareness training.

Acknowledging a policy confirms that it has been reviewed. It
does not submit a leave request, approve an absence, or change
an employee's entitlement.

If a policy acknowledgment is missing from the checklist,
the employee should contact HR.

## Annual refresher training

Employees must complete an annual security awareness refresher.
HR communicates the assignment and its deadline through the portal.

The standard completion period is 20 working days from the
assignment date. Employees who completed initial security
awareness training within the preceding 90 calendar days are
exempt from that refresher assignment.

HR confirms the exemption in the training record. Employees
must not assume that an exemption applies without confirmation.

The refresher requires completion of all modules and an assessment
score of at least 80 percent.

## Role-specific training

Some roles require additional training before particular tasks
or permissions can be assigned.

The manager and the relevant training owner identify these
requirements and communicate the course, deadline, assessment,
and completion evidence in the assignment.

This policy does not define a universal deadline or passing score
for every role-specific course.

Completing a course does not automatically grant system access.
The application owner must approve permissions through the
Technology Support process.

Employees must not perform a restricted task merely because
they have submitted a training completion request.

## Assessment attempts

Employees have up to three attempts to pass the initial security
assessment or annual refresher assessment.

After an unsuccessful attempt, employees should review the course
material before trying again.

If the third attempt is unsuccessful, the employee must contact
HR. HR coordinates additional guidance with the training owner,
who determines the next assessment arrangement.

An unsuccessful assessment is not automatically recorded as
a completed course. This policy does not prescribe an automatic
disciplinary action for an unsuccessful attempt.

## Extensions and approved absences

Employees who anticipate missing a deadline must contact HR and
their manager before the deadline, explain the blocker, and
identify the affected assignment.

The training owner must confirm any extension. A request or
manager acknowledgment alone does not change the deadline.

If an approved absence prevents completion, HR coordinates a
revised deadline with the training owner. Employees must not
perform training while recorded as medically unable to work
without first consulting HR.

The revised date must appear in the assignment record before
the employee treats it as the new deadline.

## Technical problems

Login failures, unavailable course pages, and assessment submission
errors must be reported to Technology Support.

Employees should include the course name, the time of the error,
and a description that does not expose passwords or sensitive
information.

Employees must also notify HR if the problem threatens a deadline.
A technical ticket does not automatically create an extension.

If an assessment result appears incorrect because of a submission
failure, the training owner reviews the evidence. Technology
Support cannot independently award a passing score.

## Completion records

The learning platform records completed modules and assessment
results. HR uses those records to monitor assignments.

Employees should check that their assignment shows completed
after satisfying all requirements.

If the record remains incomplete after two working days,
the employee should contact HR with the assignment reference.
HR coordinates verification with the training owner.

Employees must not upload altered completion certificates or
mark incomplete activities as complete.

## Support boundaries

HR handles assignment records, exemptions, completion corrections,
and coordination of extension requests.

Training owners handle course requirements, assessment decisions,
and extension approval. Managers arrange time for training.
Technology Support handles technical failures.

The support assistant may explain documented requirements and
identify the responsible team. It cannot grant an exemption,
change a score, approve an extension, confirm personal completion,
or authorize system access.
