# Employee Offboarding Procedure

Document ID: HR-010
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure describes administrative coordination when a
full-time employee leaves AR Cloud.

It covers departure confirmation, handover, equipment return,
access changes, and employee record closure. Contractors follow
the process coordinated by their contract owner.

This fictional procedure does not define legal notice periods,
termination grounds, severance entitlement, or final payment
amounts.

## Departure notification

Employees intending to resign must notify their direct manager
and HR in writing through the designated confidential HR channel.

The notification should include the proposed final working date.
That proposed date is not treated as confirmed until HR reviews
the applicable agreement and communicates the recorded departure
arrangement.

Employees must contact HR to clarify notice requirements for
their individual circumstances. This document does not establish
a universal notice period.

A conversation with a colleague or an out-of-office message does
not replace formal notification.

## Departure confirmation

HR confirms the final working date, the employee's administrative
contact, and the offboarding checklist.

If a departure is initiated by the company, authorized HR staff
communicate the arrangement through the appropriate confidential
process. Managers must not use this document as authorization
to make a termination decision.

Changes to a confirmed departure date require a new HR
confirmation. Employees and managers must not independently
change the date in technical access requests.

## Responsibilities

HR coordinates the administrative checklist, departure records,
and communication between departments.

The direct manager organizes the work handover and identifies
the colleague responsible for outstanding tasks.

Technology Support coordinates equipment return and account
access changes according to the confirmed schedule.

Finance handles final payment questions, outstanding expense
claims, and payment-related documentation.

The employee completes assigned handover and return activities
and reports blockers to the responsible owner.

## Work handover

The manager and employee agree a handover plan within two working
days after HR confirms the departure arrangement, or sooner
if the remaining working period is shorter.

The plan identifies open tasks, relevant deadlines, documentation
locations, and the colleague receiving each responsibility.

Employees must store company work in approved repositories or
shared locations. They must not transfer company information
to personal accounts to preserve access after departure.

Passwords must not be included in handover documents. Technology
Support manages any required ownership or permission changes.

## Equipment return

Technology Support provides an equipment inventory and return
instructions. Employees should report missing or incorrect
inventory items before arranging the return.

Unless Technology Support confirms another date, equipment must
be handed over or dispatched through the approved return method
by the final working day.

Remote employees receive instructions for the approved shipping
or collection arrangement. They must not choose an alternative
shipping method and assume that its cost is reimbursable.

Technology Support confirms receipt and reports unresolved items
to HR. Dispatching equipment does not by itself confirm receipt
or completion of the return.

## Account and permission changes

Technology Support schedules account deactivation using the
departure date and access instructions confirmed by HR.

Earlier access restrictions require a separate authorized
instruction. Employees must not assume that access will remain
available after their final working day.

Application ownership transfers require the relevant application
owner's approval. Managers identify business continuity needs
but cannot bypass the technical approval process.

The support assistant cannot deactivate accounts or confirm
the actual status of an employee's access.

## Expenses and final payments

Employees should submit outstanding expense claims before their
final working day whenever possible.

Each claim must follow the applicable Finance procedure and
include the required approval references and receipts.

HR confirmation of departure does not approve an expense claim
or establish the amount or date of a final payment.

Employees must contact Finance about payment timing, outstanding
reimbursements, and payment documentation. Finance coordinates
with HR when employee record information is required.

This document does not specify how unused vacation is treated
in a final payment. HR must confirm the applicable arrangement
rather than applying the annual carryover rules automatically.

## Benefits and departure records

HR confirms how the departure affects enrolled benefits and
communicates any applicable end dates.

Employees must not assume that the annual allowances described
in the Employee Benefits Policy, document HR-005, remain available
after employment ends.

This procedure does not define a universal benefit end date
or entitlement to unused allowance payments.

Employees requesting employment confirmation documents should
contact HR through the designated channel. HR explains the
available document and review requirements.

## Exit feedback

HR offers an optional exit feedback discussion before the final
working day when scheduling permits.

Employees may decline the discussion without treating the
offboarding checklist as incomplete.

Sensitive feedback must use the confidential HR channel.
It must not be included in general handover documents.

Submitting feedback does not guarantee a particular investigation
outcome or change the confirmed departure arrangement.

## Checklist closure and support

Each department confirms completion of its own checklist items.
HR closes the administrative checklist after required owners
have confirmed their tasks.

An unresolved equipment return, payment question, or access task
must retain an assigned owner. Checklist closure does not replace
confirmation of an individual payment or equipment receipt.

The support assistant may explain the procedure and identify
the responsible department. It cannot confirm a departure date,
calculate final payments, inspect records, authorize exceptions,
or close an employee's checklist.
