# Employee Onboarding Procedure

Document ID: HR-003
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure describes the onboarding process for new full-time
AR Cloud employees, from preparation before their start date to
the completion of their first 30 calendar days.

Contractors follow a separate process coordinated by their contract
owner. This document does not define contractor requirements.

The procedure is fictional and intended for an educational project.
It does not establish legal requirements or replace an employment
agreement.

## Responsibilities

HR coordinates administrative preparation, orientation sessions,
and the onboarding checklist.

The direct manager defines the employee's initial responsibilities,
assigns an onboarding buddy, and schedules progress meetings.

Technology Support provisions equipment and approved system access.
HR can coordinate an access request but cannot troubleshoot
technical problems or grant permissions.

The employee completes assigned activities, reports blockers,
and keeps their onboarding checklist updated.

## Preparation before the start date

HR sends the welcome email at least five working days before the
confirmed start date. It includes the first-day schedule, the HR
contact, and instructions for submitting administrative documents.

Employees must submit the requested documents through the secure
HR submission link. They must not send identity documents or bank
details to public channels or team mailing lists.

If the welcome email has not arrived three working days before
the start date, the employee should contact HR.

If the secure submission link does not work, the employee should
notify HR. HR determines whether the link must be reissued or
whether Technology Support needs to investigate a technical issue.

## Equipment and initial access

The manager submits the equipment and access request to Technology
Support at least five working days before the employee starts.
The request identifies the role, required applications, and
designated permission approvers.

Technology Support confirms delivery or collection arrangements
directly with the employee. Remote employees must report missing
equipment to Technology Support and notify their manager.

New employees must activate their company account and configure
multi-factor authentication using the instructions provided by
Technology Support.

An onboarding checklist does not authorize access by itself.
Permissions remain subject to the relevant application owner's
approval.

## First-day activities

HR conducts an orientation covering company structure, HR support
channels, and the location of internal policies.

The manager explains the employee's responsibilities, introduces
the team, and reviews the first week's schedule.

The onboarding buddy helps the employee locate documentation and
understand routine team practices. The buddy cannot approve leave,
change HR records, or grant system access.

If an employee cannot attend the scheduled orientation, they must
contact HR to arrange another session. Missing the session does
not automatically cancel or change the employment start date.

## First-week requirements

Employees must complete the assigned security awareness training
within five working days of their start date.

They must also review the Vacation Policy, document HR-001, and
the Leave Policy, document HR-002, and acknowledge them in the
employee portal.

Acknowledging a policy confirms that it has been reviewed.
It does not submit a vacation or leave request.

Employees must confirm that they can access the tools required
for their role. Technical access failures belong to Technology
Support; questions about the onboarding schedule belong to HR
or the manager.

If a technical blocker prevents training completion, the employee
must notify Technology Support and HR before the deadline.
An extension requires confirmation from the training owner.

## Progress meetings

The manager holds a first-week check-in by the end of the
employee's fifth working day. The meeting covers access blockers,
role expectations, and the initial work plan.

A second check-in takes place by the end of the employee's first
30 calendar days. The manager reviews completed onboarding tasks,
remaining support needs, and priorities for the following month.

These meetings are onboarding reviews. They do not establish
a probation outcome, compensation change, or promotion decision.

## Checklist completion

HR maintains the administrative checklist, Technology Support
confirms technical provisioning tasks, and the manager confirms
role-specific activities.

An item is complete only when its designated owner confirms it.
Employees must not mark missing equipment or unavailable access
as complete to close the checklist.

HR closes the onboarding checklist once all required items have
been confirmed. Unresolved items remain open with an assigned
owner and expected resolution date.

## Changes to the start date

Employees must contact HR if they need to request a different
start date. HR coordinates the request with the manager and
confirms any approved change in writing.

Until that confirmation is received, the existing start date
remains in effect. Technology Support adjusts provisioning
arrangements after receiving the confirmed change.

## Support boundaries

HR handles orientation, administrative documents, and checklist
coordination. Managers handle role expectations and work planning.
Technology Support handles equipment, account activation,
authentication, and technical access failures.

The support assistant may explain the procedure and identify
the responsible department. It cannot activate accounts, verify
submitted documents, approve permissions, or close onboarding
tasks.
