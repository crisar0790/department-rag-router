# Performance Review Procedure

Document ID: HR-008
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure describes the performance review process for
full-time AR Cloud employees.

Its purpose is to review agreed objectives, discuss documented
feedback, and establish a development plan.

Contractors follow the review arrangements in their individual
agreements. This fictional procedure does not define employment
law, disciplinary requirements, or compensation entitlements.

## Review schedule

AR Cloud conducts two regular performance review cycles each year,
in June and December.

HR announces the opening date, submission deadlines, and meeting
period for each cycle at least 15 working days before it begins.

Employees who have completed at least 90 calendar days of employment
by the cycle's opening date participate in that cycle.

Employees below that threshold continue with onboarding check-ins
under the Employee Onboarding Procedure, document HR-003, and join
the next regular cycle when eligible.

## Responsibilities

HR coordinates the cycle, provides the review forms, and monitors
completion.

The direct manager assesses agreed objectives, gathers relevant
feedback, and conducts the review meeting.

The employee completes a self-assessment, provides examples of
work performed, and participates in the development discussion.

Managers must use documented evidence relevant to the review
period. A review must not be based solely on an isolated comment
or an undocumented impression.

## Objectives and evidence

Managers and employees record role objectives at the beginning
of the review period. Each objective should describe the expected
result, its measurement, and its target date.

If responsibilities change during the period, the manager and
employee must record the revised objectives and the effective
date.

The review considers the objectives that applied during each
part of the period. Employees should not be assessed against
an objective introduced only after the relevant work was completed.

Examples of evidence include completed deliverables, documented
service improvements, agreed operational metrics, and feedback
connected to specific work.

## Self-assessment

Employees submit their self-assessment through the employee portal
within five working days of the cycle's opening date.

The self-assessment should summarize progress against objectives,
important contributions, challenges, and development needs.

Employees should reference work evidence without copying sensitive
customer information or confidential employee records into
the form.

If an employee cannot meet the deadline, they must contact their
manager and HR before it expires. An extension requires explicit
confirmation.

A technical portal failure should be reported to Technology Support
and HR. The employee must not assume that the deadline changes
automatically.

## Manager assessment and meeting

Managers submit their assessment within five working days after
receiving the employee's self-assessment.

The review meeting should take place within 10 working days after
the manager assessment is submitted.

The meeting covers results, differences between the assessments,
support needs, and development priorities.

The manager explains the evidence supporting the assessment and
records relevant employee comments. Employees may request
clarification when a conclusion is unclear or lacks an example.

If the manager is unavailable, HR coordinates an alternative
review arrangement rather than treating the assessment as complete.

## Review outcomes

The assessment uses three outcome categories: exceeds expectations,
meets expectations, and needs improvement.

An exceeds-expectations outcome indicates that documented results
went beyond the agreed role objectives in a sustained way.

A meets-expectations outcome indicates that the employee fulfilled
the agreed objectives and role requirements.

A needs-improvement outcome identifies documented gaps requiring
additional support, clearer objectives, or a development plan.

These categories do not automatically determine salary changes,
promotions, disciplinary actions, or termination decisions.
Those matters require separate procedures and authorized decisions.

## Development plan

The manager and employee record a development plan within five
working days after the review meeting.

The plan identifies specific actions, responsible participants,
target dates, and a follow-up meeting.

If an action involves a course or certification, funding follows
the Employee Benefits Policy, document HR-005. Including a course
in a development plan does not itself approve a purchase.

Changes to responsibilities or working arrangements must follow
their applicable approval procedures.

## Acknowledgment and disagreement

Employees acknowledge receipt of the review in the portal within
five working days after the meeting.

Acknowledgment confirms that the review was received and discussed.
It does not mean that the employee agrees with every conclusion.

Employees may record a disagreement and request HR review within
10 working days after the meeting. The request should identify
the disputed conclusion and relevant evidence.

HR acknowledges the request within three working days and identifies
the appropriate reviewer. This document does not guarantee a
fixed resolution time or a changed outcome.

## Access and corrections

Review records are available to the employee, the responsible
manager, and authorized HR reviewers.

Employees must report factual errors to HR with the review reference
and the information requiring correction.

Technical failures opening a review belong to Technology Support.
Questions about assessment evidence or conclusions belong to
the manager and HR.

## Support boundaries

The support assistant may explain schedules, eligibility,
submission requirements, outcome categories, and disagreement
procedures.

It cannot inspect an individual's review, assign a rating,
approve an extension, change a review record, or promise a
promotion or compensation adjustment.
