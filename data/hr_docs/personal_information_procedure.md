# Personal Information Update Procedure

Document ID: HR-006
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how full-time AR Cloud employees request
updates to their personal information in the employee portal.

It covers contact details, home addresses, emergency contacts,
legal names, and bank details used for employee payments.
Contractors must contact their contract owner about changes
to their records.

The procedure is fictional and intended for an educational project.
It does not define legal requirements or authorize the support
assistant to access personal records.

## Employee responsibilities

Employees must keep their personal information accurate and report
changes within five working days whenever possible.

A change request must identify the record category and the effective
date. Employees should provide only the information necessary
for that update.

Submitting a request does not mean that the record has already
changed. Employees must check the confirmation and review the
updated record after processing.

If a request is urgent, employees should contact HR with the
request reference and explain the timing requirement. Urgent
processing is not guaranteed.

## Contact details

Employees may update their personal telephone number and personal
email address in the portal's profile section.

The portal may require verification before accepting a new
personal email address. Employees must complete the verification
instructions sent to that address.

Changing a personal email address does not change the company
account address or login identifier.

Requests to change a company account identifier belong to
Technology Support and may require HR confirmation of the
underlying employee record.

## Home address changes

Employees must submit a home address change through the portal,
including the date on which the new address becomes effective.

HR reviews the request and may ask for supporting documentation
through the confidential upload section. This document does not
establish a universal documentation requirement for every
address change.

Updating an address does not approve a new remote working location.
Employees with a remote arrangement must also follow the Remote
Work Policy, document HR-004.

An international address change requires HR review before any
assumption about employment or remote work arrangements.

## Emergency contacts

Employees should maintain at least one emergency contact in the
portal. Each entry must include the contact's name, relationship,
and telephone number.

Employees must ensure that the person knows they have been listed
as an emergency contact and that the telephone number is current.

Emergency contacts do not receive access to the employee portal.
Listing a person does not authorize routine disclosure of employee
records or make that person a benefits beneficiary.

Questions about beneficiary designation require a separate
discussion with HR and are outside this procedure.

## Legal name changes

Employees requesting a legal name change must submit a confidential
HR request through the portal.

HR explains which supporting documents are required for the
employee's circumstances. Employees must upload those documents
only through the designated confidential channel.

HR confirms when the administrative record has been updated.
Changes to company account names, email addresses, or application
profiles may require additional Technology Support tasks.

The HR confirmation does not mean that every technical system
has already been updated.

## Bank detail changes

Employees must submit bank detail changes through the portal's
secure payment-details section. Bank information must not be
included in ordinary support tickets, team messages, or public
channels.

HR coordinates verification of the employee record. Finance
reviews payment-related requirements and confirms whether the
change can apply to an upcoming payment.

This document does not define a universal payment cutoff date.
Employees must contact Finance to confirm the timing for their
specific request.

A submitted change does not guarantee that an upcoming payment
will use the new details. Employees must wait for confirmation
before treating the change as effective.

## Review and processing

HR normally reviews personal information requests within three
working days. This is a review target, not a guarantee that every
change will be completed within that period.

Requests requiring verification, additional documents, or Finance
coordination may take longer. HR communicates any missing
information through the portal.

If a request has not received an initial response after three
working days, the employee should contact HR with its reference.
Employees should not submit duplicate requests merely to restart
the review process.

## Corrections and technical problems

If an updated record contains an error, employees must report it
to HR with the original request reference and the field requiring
correction.

If the portal cannot be accessed or a form fails to load, the
employee should contact Technology Support. HR should also be
notified if the technical issue blocks a time-sensitive update.

Employees must not use an unapproved channel to bypass secure
submission requirements. HR will explain an approved alternative
when the portal cannot be used.

## Support boundaries

HR handles personal record changes, required documentation,
verification coordination, and administrative corrections.

Finance handles payment timing and payment-related bank
requirements. Technology Support handles login failures, portal
errors, and technical account changes.

The support assistant may explain the procedure and identify
the responsible department. It cannot verify identity, inspect
bank details, modify records, or confirm an individual's
payment destination.
