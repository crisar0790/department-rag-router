# Remote Work Policy

Document ID: HR-004
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This policy describes remote and hybrid work arrangements for
full-time AR Cloud employees. Eligibility depends on the role,
operational requirements, and the employee's approved arrangement.

Contractors follow their individual agreements. This fictional
policy is intended for an educational project and does not
represent employment law.

## Eligibility and approval

Employees may request a recurring remote or hybrid arrangement
after discussing it with their direct manager.

The request must identify the proposed work location, remote
working days, working hours, and any equipment requirements.
Employees submit the request through the employee portal.

The manager reviews operational coverage and role requirements.
HR confirms the approved arrangement and records it in the portal.
A manager's informal agreement does not replace HR confirmation.

Employees must continue following their existing arrangement
until the change is confirmed. Remote work is not automatically
available to every role.

## Occasional remote days

Employees may request an occasional remote day without changing
their recurring arrangement. They must obtain manager approval
at least one working day before the requested date.

Unexpected circumstances may justify a shorter notice period,
but approval is still required. Employees unable to work because
of illness should follow the Leave Policy, document HR-002,
rather than treating the absence as a remote working day.

An occasional remote day does not create an ongoing entitlement
to work remotely.

## Working hours and availability

Remote employees follow the working hours recorded in their
approved arrangement. A change of location does not automatically
change working hours or reduce expected availability.

Employees must attend scheduled meetings, keep their calendar
updated, and tell their manager about interruptions affecting
their availability.

Teams may define agreed collaboration periods within approved
working hours. Managers must communicate those periods clearly
and consult HR if they conflict with an employee's recorded
arrangement.

Remote work does not require continuous availability outside
approved working hours. Questions about schedules belong to the
manager or HR.

## Approved locations

Employees must work from the location recorded in their approved
arrangement. A permanent change of home address must be reported
to HR through the employee portal.

Employees wishing to work temporarily from another location
within their employment country must request manager approval
at least five working days in advance.

Working from another country requires a separate HR review.
Employees must submit the proposed country, dates, and business
reason at least 20 working days before travel.

International remote work is not approved automatically.
Employees must wait for written HR confirmation before making
work arrangements based on the request.

This policy does not guarantee approval or establish a universal
maximum duration for international remote work.

## Equipment and technical support

Technology Support manages company equipment, approved software,
secure connections, and technical troubleshooting.

Employees must use company-approved devices for work and follow
the technical security instructions provided for their role.
They must not install unapproved software to bypass an access
problem.

Missing equipment, VPN failures, and authentication problems
belong to Technology Support. HR handles questions about remote
work eligibility and approved locations.

Employees must report lost or stolen company equipment to
Technology Support immediately and notify their manager.

## Internet and work interruptions

Employees are responsible for arranging a suitable internet
connection at their approved remote location.

If an outage prevents work, employees must notify their manager
as soon as possible and provide an estimated restoration time
when available.

The manager determines whether an alternative approved location
or a temporary work adjustment is appropriate. Employees must
not assume that an outage automatically counts as paid leave
or vacation.

Recurring connectivity problems require a discussion with the
manager about the suitability of the remote arrangement.

## Expenses and reimbursement

This policy does not authorize reimbursement for internet,
furniture, coworking spaces, or other remote work expenses.

Employees must follow the Finance expense procedure and obtain
any required approval before making a purchase. Approval to work
remotely does not itself approve an expense.

Questions about reimbursement eligibility, documentation, or
payment status belong to Finance.

## Review and changes

Managers and HR may review a remote arrangement when role
requirements, staffing needs, or the approved location change.

HR communicates confirmed changes in writing, including the
effective date. Employees should contact HR if a communicated
change conflicts with their recorded arrangement or agreement.

Employees requesting a return to office-based work must discuss
the change with their manager and submit it through the portal.
Office availability and equipment requirements must be confirmed
before the change takes effect.

## Support boundaries

HR handles eligibility, recorded arrangements, address updates,
and international location reviews. Managers handle operational
coverage, schedules, and occasional remote-day approval.

Technology Support handles devices, software, VPN access, and
technical incidents. Finance handles expense approval and
reimbursement procedures.

The support assistant may explain these responsibilities.
It cannot approve remote work, authorize international work,
grant technical access, or approve reimbursement.
