# Vacation Policy

Document ID: HR-001
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how eligible AR Cloud employees request, use,
and cancel annual vacation. It applies to full-time employees.
Contractors follow the time-off terms in their individual agreements
and must contact their contract owner for clarification.

The rules describe a fictional company policy. They are not legal
guidance or a statement of employment law in any jurisdiction.

## Annual entitlement

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of vacation per calendar
year. Saturdays, Sundays, and company holidays do not count as
vacation days for employees following the standard Monday-to-Friday
schedule.

Employees joining during the year receive a prorated entitlement
based on their start date. HR records the calculated entitlement in
the employee portal. Employees must use the portal to check their
personal balance; this document cannot establish an individual's
available days.

Part-time employees must contact HR to confirm their entitlement
under their employment agreement.

## Request procedure

Employees submit vacation requests through the employee portal.
Each request must include the first day of absence, the last day
of absence, and the colleague responsible for urgent handover items.

Requests must be submitted at least 10 working days before the
first day of vacation. Employees should discuss planned absences
with their manager before submitting the request, especially when
the dates overlap with a product release or another team member's
approved absence.

The direct manager approves or rejects the request in the portal.
A conversation, calendar entry, or email does not replace portal
approval. Employees must not assume that a pending request has
been approved.

## Approval and scheduling

Managers should review requests within three working days.
Approval depends on the available balance, existing approved
absences, and minimum staffing requirements.

If a request remains pending after three working days, the employee
should contact the manager. If the manager is unavailable, the
employee should contact HR, which will identify an alternative
approver. HR does not automatically approve a request because the
review deadline has passed.

When overlapping requests cannot all be approved, managers consider
the order of submission and operational coverage. Employees whose
requests are rejected may propose alternative dates.

## Short-notice requests

Employees may request an exception to the 10-working-day notice
period for unexpected personal circumstances. They must explain
the reason to their manager and submit the request in the portal.

An exception requires explicit manager approval. It is not
guaranteed and does not increase the employee's vacation balance.
Medical absences and other protected leave categories must follow
their separate policies rather than being automatically classified
as vacation.

## Carryover and expiration

Employees may carry over a maximum of five unused vacation days
into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by
March 31 of that year.

The portal applies carried-over days before the current year's
entitlement. Days above the carryover limit expire unless HR has
documented an exception because approved vacation could not be
taken for company operational reasons.

Employees should raise a possible carryover exception with HR
before December 15. Managers cannot grant a carryover exception
without HR confirmation.

## Changes and cancellations

Employees must request changes or cancellations through the portal.
Changed dates require a new approval decision, even when the
number of vacation days remains the same.

Cancelled days return to the available balance after the cancellation
is processed. Employees should contact HR if the balance has not
updated within two working days.

## Handover and support

Before an approved absence, employees must document urgent tasks,
share the handover with their designated colleague, and configure
an out-of-office message.

Questions about approval belong to the direct manager. Questions
about entitlement, balance corrections, carryover exceptions, or
portal records belong to HR. Technical problems accessing the
portal belong to Technology Support.
