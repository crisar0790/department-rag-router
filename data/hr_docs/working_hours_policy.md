# Working Hours and Time Recording Policy

Document ID: HR-011
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how full-time AR Cloud employees confirm
their working schedules, record working time, and request schedule
changes.

It applies to employees working in the office, remotely, or under
a hybrid arrangement. Contractors follow their individual
agreements.

This fictional policy does not establish statutory working-hour
limits, overtime rates, or legal rest requirements. Questions
about individual contractual conditions belong to HR.

## Recorded working schedule

Each employee's approved working schedule is recorded in the
employee portal. The record identifies working days, start and
finish times, breaks, and the applicable time zone.

Employees must review the schedule during onboarding and contact
HR if it differs from the arrangement communicated to them.

This document does not define one universal schedule for all
employees. The support assistant cannot determine an individual's
hours without access to an authorized employee record.

A remote working arrangement does not automatically change the
recorded schedule, as explained in the Remote Work Policy,
document HR-004.

## Schedule change requests

Employees requesting a recurring schedule change must discuss
the proposal with their manager and submit a request through
the employee portal at least 10 working days before the proposed
effective date.

The request must identify the proposed working days, hours,
time zone, effective date, and reason for the change.

The manager reviews operational coverage. HR checks the employee
record and confirms any approved arrangement in writing.

Until confirmation is received, the current recorded schedule
remains in effect. A calendar update or informal conversation
does not replace approval.

## Temporary adjustments

Employees may request a temporary adjustment for a specific
working day by contacting their manager at least one working
day in advance when the circumstances are foreseeable.

The request must state the affected date, proposed hours, and
how scheduled responsibilities will be covered.

Unexpected circumstances may justify shorter notice, but the
employee must notify the manager as soon as possible.

A temporary adjustment does not create a permanent schedule
change. If the adjustment affects contracted hours or extends
beyond five consecutive working days, HR must review it before
it is treated as an approved arrangement.

## Time recording

Employees record working time in the employee portal by the end
of each working day.

Entries must identify the date, hours worked, and the appropriate
activity category. Employees must not record hours they did
not work or copy another employee's entries.

Approved vacation and leave must be recorded under their
corresponding absence categories rather than as working time.

Mandatory training completed during approved working hours
counts as working time. Employees should use the designated
training category.

If a required category is missing, employees should contact HR
rather than select an unrelated category to submit the entry.

## Breaks and interruptions

Employees follow the breaks recorded in their approved schedule
and any additional requirements confirmed by HR.

This document does not establish a universal break duration
or determine whether a particular break is paid.

Employees should discuss planned interruptions with their
manager. Unexpected interruptions affecting availability must
be reported as soon as possible.

An interruption does not automatically become vacation, leave,
or additional working time. The relevant arrangement must be
confirmed and recorded correctly.

## Additional hours

Employees must obtain manager approval before working additional
hours beyond their recorded schedule.

The request must identify the task, proposed additional hours,
and the reason the work cannot be completed within the existing
schedule.

Manager approval authorizes the operational request. It does
not independently establish an overtime payment rate or a
compensatory time entitlement.

HR confirms how approved additional hours should be recorded.
Finance handles questions about confirmed payment processing.

If an urgent incident requires unplanned additional work,
employees must notify their manager as soon as possible and
submit the actual hours for review. They must not assume that
all unplanned hours are automatically approved.

## Corrections and review

Employees requesting a time-entry correction must use the
portal's correction function and explain the original entry,
the correct value, and the reason.

The manager reviews corrections affecting recorded working
hours. HR handles corrections to absence categories or
administrative schedule records.

Managers should review submitted corrections within three
working days. This is a review target, not a guarantee that
every related payment adjustment will be completed within
that period.

Approved corrections remain visible in the record history.
Employees must not delete supporting information to conceal
an earlier incorrect entry.

## Missing entries and technical failures

If an employee discovers a missing entry, they should submit
it promptly and notify their manager if the reporting period
has already closed.

HR explains the correction process for a closed period.
Employees must not assume that a late entry automatically
changes a completed payment calculation.

Portal login failures, unavailable forms, and submission errors
belong to Technology Support. Employees must also notify HR
if a technical problem blocks a time-sensitive correction.

A technical ticket does not replace the time entry or establish
the number of hours worked.

## Support boundaries

Managers handle operational schedule requests, additional-hour
approval, and working-time corrections.

HR handles recorded schedules, absence classifications,
contractual questions, and closed-period correction procedures.
Finance handles payment processing questions. Technology Support
handles technical access and submission failures.

The support assistant may explain these procedures. It cannot
inspect personal time records, approve additional hours, calculate
overtime pay, change schedules, or submit corrections.
