# Workplace Adjustments and Accessibility Procedure

Document ID: HR-013
Department: HR
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic procedure for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how full-time AR Cloud employees request
workplace adjustments that help them participate in their work
and access company activities.

Requests may concern workstation arrangements, accessible tools,
meeting formats, communication methods, or working arrangements.

Contractors must contact their contract owner, who coordinates
the appropriate review with HR and the relevant operational team.

This fictional procedure is intended for an educational project.
It does not define legal eligibility, provide medical advice,
or establish a universal entitlement to a particular adjustment.
Individual circumstances require review by authorized staff.

## Examples of workplace adjustments

An adjustment request may involve an alternative keyboard,
a different display arrangement, an accessible document format,
captions for meetings, or changes to how instructions are provided.

Other requests may concern a quieter workspace, an alternative
meeting location, or a temporary change to an agreed schedule.

These examples illustrate possible requests. They are not
automatically approved benefits or a complete list of available
options.

Employees do not need to identify the final technical solution
before contacting HR. They may describe the barrier and the
work activity affected so that the appropriate teams can review
possible approaches.

## Request channel

Employees submit requests through the employee portal's
confidential HR request channel.

The initial request should describe the work barrier, the tasks
or activities affected, any proposed adjustment, and whether
the need is temporary or ongoing.

Employees should identify an upcoming deadline when the barrier
affects a scheduled activity, such as required training or
a performance review meeting.

If the portal is inaccessible, employees should contact HR
through the confidential alternative channel provided during
onboarding. HR confirms an approved submission method.

Technical access failures should also be reported to Technology
Support. Employees should describe the technical failure without
including confidential supporting documents in the technical ticket.

## Information and confidentiality

Employees should provide only the information needed to explain
the work barrier and support the review.

A diagnosis is not required in the initial request. HR determines
whether additional supporting information is needed and explains
the purpose and approved submission method.

Medical information must use the confidential upload channel.
It must not be included in team messages, ordinary support tickets,
meeting invitations, or shared handover documents.

Managers and implementation teams receive the functional
requirements needed to arrange the adjustment. They do not
automatically receive all supporting information submitted to HR.

HR explains who needs access to the request and how the review
will be coordinated. The support assistant must not promise
absolute confidentiality or inspect personal supporting documents.

## Initial review

HR acknowledges a request within two working days during
the support hours defined in the HR Support and Escalation
Procedure, document HR-007.

The acknowledgment identifies the request owner and the next
review step. It does not confirm approval or implementation.

HR aims to hold an initial discussion with the employee within
five working days after receiving a sufficiently detailed request.

If more information is required, HR identifies the missing items
and explains how to submit them. The request remains open while
the information is being collected.

The initial discussion explores the affected tasks, existing
arrangements, possible options, and any time-sensitive needs.

## Review responsibilities

HR coordinates the request and confirms the approved arrangement.

The direct manager provides information about task requirements,
team coverage, and operational scheduling.

Technology Support evaluates technical compatibility, accessibility
features, installation requirements, and security implications.

The workplace coordinator evaluates office space, furniture,
physical access, and onsite implementation requirements.

Finance reviews purchasing and payment requirements when an
approved adjustment involves expenditure. Finance does not decide
whether an employee's request should be approved.

When several teams are involved, HR assigns an owner to each
implementation task and communicates the coordination plan.

## Temporary arrangements during review

An employee may ask HR whether a temporary arrangement is possible
while the full request is being reviewed.

The request should identify the immediate barrier and the
proposed temporary solution.

HR coordinates with the manager and relevant implementation team
before confirming any temporary arrangement.

Employees must not install unapproved software, purchase equipment,
or change their recorded schedule on the assumption that
a temporary arrangement will later be approved.

Temporary approval identifies its scope, effective date, review
date, and any conditions. It does not automatically establish
permanent approval.

If no temporary solution is confirmed, HR explains the next
review step. This document does not guarantee immediate
implementation of every request.

## Decision and written confirmation

HR communicates the decision in writing through the confidential
request channel.

For an approved adjustment, the confirmation describes the
agreed measures, effective date, implementation owners, and
follow-up arrangements.

If implementation depends on procurement or technical work,
the confirmation distinguishes approval from availability.
An approved item may still be awaiting delivery or configuration.

If the requested solution is not approved, HR explains the
decision and identifies any alternative option offered.

This procedure does not establish one universal decision deadline.
HR communicates an estimate based on the complexity of the
request and updates it if required dependencies change.

## Equipment and software implementation

Technology Support coordinates approved technical equipment
and software through the established provisioning process.

Employees must wait for installation and configuration instructions
before using a new tool with company information.

Approval of an adjustment does not authorize bypassing security
controls, sharing accounts, or granting additional application
permissions.

If a proposed accessibility tool conflicts with a security
requirement, Technology Support explains the constraint to HR
and helps assess an alternative that addresses the work barrier.

Employees should report installation problems or failures in
an approved tool to Technology Support. HR must also be notified
when the failure prevents the agreed adjustment from functioning.

## Purchasing and reimbursement

Employees must not purchase equipment or services intended for
reimbursement before the required approval has been confirmed.

The written arrangement identifies whether the company will
purchase the item directly or whether an approved employee
purchase is permitted.

For an approved employee purchase, Finance confirms receipt
requirements, the approved spending limit, and the payment process.

Workplace adjustments are reviewed separately from the wellness
and professional development allowances described in the Employee
Benefits Policy, document HR-005.

An approved adjustment is not automatically deducted from those
allowances. Any financial arrangement must be stated explicitly
in the approval.

This procedure does not specify a universal reimbursement date,
currency conversion rule, or ownership arrangement for every item.

## Meetings and accessible information

Employees may request meeting captions, accessible presentation
materials, an alternative document format, or another communication
method through HR.

For a known upcoming event, employees should submit the request
at least five working days before the event when possible.

Shorter notice does not automatically invalidate a request.
HR coordinates with the event owner to assess what can be
provided within the available time.

The event owner arranges approved materials and communication
measures. Technology Support assists with technical features.

Meeting notes must not disclose the employee's confidential
supporting information. Only the practical arrangements needed
for participation should be communicated to attendees.

## Training and assessment adjustments

Employees facing an accessibility barrier in mandatory training
should contact HR and identify the course and affected activity.

HR coordinates accessible delivery options with the training
owner and Technology Support.

An approved alternative format does not automatically change
the passing score or remove a required assessment.

If the barrier threatens a completion deadline, an extension
must be confirmed by the training owner under the Mandatory
Training Policy, document HR-009.

Employees must not assume that an open adjustment request pauses
the training deadline. HR communicates any confirmed interim
arrangement and the revised date when applicable.

## Schedule and location changes

Adjustments involving working hours must be coordinated with HR
and the manager and recorded in the employee portal.

The confirmation identifies whether the arrangement is temporary
or recurring and how working time should be recorded.

A workplace adjustment request does not independently approve
remote work from a new location or another country.

Location changes must also follow the Remote Work Policy,
document HR-004, with HR coordinating the related approvals.

Employees should ask HR to resolve any conflict between the
adjustment confirmation and an existing recorded arrangement
before assuming that one silently replaces the other.

## Follow-up and effectiveness

HR schedules a follow-up discussion approximately 10 working
days after the adjustment becomes available.

The discussion checks whether the agreed measures address the
reported barrier and whether additional implementation work
is needed.

An employee may report a problem earlier rather than wait for
the scheduled follow-up. The report should identify the affected
measure and its impact on the work activity.

A request is not considered successfully implemented solely
because equipment was delivered or a task was marked complete.
HR confirms the outcome with the employee and relevant owners.

## Changes, review, and closure

Employees should contact HR if their tasks, work location,
or adjustment needs change.

HR reviews whether the existing arrangement remains suitable.
Changes require written confirmation; managers must not
independently remove an approved measure.

Temporary arrangements are reviewed by their recorded review
date. A review date does not automatically mean that the
arrangement has ended.

HR confirms closure after the agreed implementation and follow-up
actions are completed. An unresolved issue retains an assigned
owner and a communicated next step.

## Disagreement and support boundaries

Employees who disagree with a decision may request review through
the confidential HR channel, identifying the disputed decision
and relevant additional information.

HR acknowledges the review request within three working days
and identifies an appropriate reviewer. A review does not
guarantee approval of the originally requested solution.

The support assistant may explain submission steps, responsibilities,
and documented review targets. It cannot determine individual
eligibility, evaluate medical information, approve an adjustment,
authorize expenditure, or confirm implementation status.
