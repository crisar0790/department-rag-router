# Company Device Assignment and Maintenance Policy

Document ID: TECH-003
Department: Technology
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

AR Cloud provides managed equipment to support approved employee work.
Technology maintains the device inventory, prepares equipment, investigates
technical faults, and coordinates repairs or replacements.

This policy covers company laptops, monitors, keyboards, pointing devices,
headsets, and approved accessories. Equipment remains company property unless
a separate written agreement explicitly states otherwise.

An equipment assignment does not establish eligibility for remote work,
change an employment arrangement, or authorize an expense reimbursement.
HR owns employment arrangements, while Finance owns purchasing and payment
controls.

## Initial equipment requests

The employee's manager submits an initial equipment request at least five
working days before the employee's confirmed start date. The request includes
the employee identifier, start date, work location, role, and any documented
technical requirements.

Technology reviews the request and selects an available device suitable for
the approved role. Employees are not guaranteed a particular brand, model,
color, or operating system merely because they express a preference.

A requirement for specialized hardware must describe the business task and
receive manager approval before Technology reviews technical suitability.
Manager approval does not guarantee availability or authorize a purchase.

If a workplace adjustment requires equipment, HR coordinates the approved
functional requirement with Technology. The equipment ticket should contain
the information needed for implementation without unrelated medical details.

The five-working-day request period supports preparation. It is not a promise
that every device will arrive within five working days, especially when
shipping or specialized procurement is required.

## Preparation and delivery

Before assignment, Technology records the device in the asset inventory and
applies the approved operating system configuration, encryption, endpoint
protection, and management settings.

Employees must not remove management software, disable encryption, or replace
the operating system without written Technology authorization. Administrative
access is granted only through a separately approved access process.

Technology communicates the planned handover or shipping arrangements through
the equipment ticket. Employees should verify delivery details and report an
incorrect address before dispatch.

A shipping confirmation means that a shipment has been dispatched. It does
not prove that the employee has received the equipment or that the device is
ready for every application required by the role.

If equipment has not arrived by the communicated delivery date, the employee
or manager should update the original ticket. Technology reviews the shipment
and any temporary work arrangement with the relevant owner.

## Receipt and acceptance

Employees should inspect delivered equipment and report visible damage,
missing accessories, or an incorrect asset identifier within two working
days of receipt.

The report should include the ticket reference, the affected item, and a
description of the issue. Photographs may be included when they do not expose
confidential information.

Employees acknowledge receipt through the approved equipment record. Receipt
acknowledgement confirms possession, not that every application or permission
has been tested.

Packaging should be retained until the initial inspection is complete.
Technology may request that suitable packaging be retained longer when a
return shipment or repair is already being arranged.

If a device cannot complete its initial setup, the employee should contact
Technology rather than trying to remove management controls or use another
employee's credentials.

## Daily care and permitted use

Employees should keep equipment in a secure location and prevent unauthorized
people from using company accounts or accessing company information.

Devices should be locked when unattended and transported in suitable
protective packaging. Employees should avoid leaving equipment unattended
in vehicles or exposed to liquids, excessive heat, or other foreseeable
sources of damage.

Company equipment is assigned to the named employee. Lending it to another
person requires Technology authorization and an updated custody record.

Employees must not sell, donate, exchange, or dispose of company equipment.
A device that appears obsolete or unusable still belongs in the asset
inventory until Technology authorizes its disposition.

Only approved applications and peripherals may be used for company work.
An accessory purchased personally is not automatically approved for connection
and does not create a right to reimbursement.

## Updates and maintenance

Employees should allow scheduled operating system and security updates to
complete and restart the device when required by the approved management
notification.

A routine update restart should be completed within two working days after
the notification unless Technology communicates a different deadline.
Technology may require a shorter deadline for an urgent security update.

An employee who cannot restart because of an essential business activity
should contact Technology before the deadline and describe the conflict.
A manager's agreement alone does not create an update exemption.

Employees should keep the device connected to an approved network regularly
so that management settings and updates can be received. If updates repeatedly
fail, the employee should submit a support ticket with the visible error and
the approximate time of the failure.

Employees must not download an unofficial update package or accept help from
an unsolicited caller claiming that a device requires urgent maintenance.

## Fault reporting and troubleshooting

Hardware faults should be reported through the Technology support process.
The ticket should identify the asset, explain the symptoms, describe when
the problem began, and state how it affects work.

Employees may perform basic checks such as verifying the power connection,
checking an approved cable, or restarting when it is safe to do so. They
should stop if the device shows signs of electrical damage, unusual heat,
swelling, smoke, or liquid exposure.

A potentially unsafe device should be disconnected from power when this can
be done safely and should not be used again until Technology provides
guidance. Employees must not open a damaged battery or attempt an internal
repair.

Technology may arrange diagnostics, remote assistance, or an authorized repair.
Employees must not take company equipment to an external repair provider
without written approval.

Support priorities and initial response targets follow TECH-002. This policy
does not establish a separate guaranteed repair or replacement deadline.

## Repair, replacement, and loan devices

Technology decides whether an approved repair, a replacement, or a temporary
loan device is the appropriate technical response.

A replacement request must describe the fault or the unmet business
requirement. Device age alone does not guarantee replacement, and this policy
does not define a universal replacement cycle.

A loan device depends on available inventory. Employees should not assume
that a particular model or an immediate loan will be available.

When a loan is assigned, Technology records its asset identifier, custodian,
and expected return date. The employee should return it within three working
days after receiving the repaired or permanent replacement device, unless
Technology approves a different date in writing.

Before handing over a device for repair or exchange, employees should follow
Technology's instructions for protecting work data. They must not perform
an independent factory reset or assume that a repair includes data recovery.

## Lost or stolen equipment

Employees should report lost or stolen company equipment immediately through
the designated security reporting channel. They should not wait for regular
support hours or for confirmation that unauthorized access has occurred.

The report should identify the device, its asset identifier if known, the
approximate time and location of the loss, and whether company accounts were
signed in.

Passwords, recovery codes, and other secrets must not be included in the
report. Technology may take protective action under the incident procedure,
but an employee or assistant cannot assume that remote locking or erasure
has already occurred.

A manager should be informed about the resulting work interruption without
unnecessarily distributing sensitive incident details.

Replacement arrangements are reviewed after the report. A loss report does
not automatically approve a replacement purchase or determine employee
financial responsibility.

## Returns and offboarding

Employees leaving AR Cloud should return assigned equipment by their last
working day unless Technology approves a different arrangement in writing.

For remote returns, Technology provides the approved shipping instructions
and confirms the return destination. Employees should not ship equipment to
an address taken from an unverified message.

Employees should include assigned accessories and retain the shipment
reference. Dispatching a package does not count as confirmed receipt by
Technology.

Technology records the return after checking the received assets. Employees
should report missing return instructions before the expected return date
rather than assuming that the obligation has been cancelled.

HR confirms employment dates and offboarding arrangements. Finance handles
any payment question. Technology records equipment custody and return status
but does not calculate final salary or decide benefit eligibility.

## Assistant limitations

The assistant can explain equipment procedures and identify the information
required for a request. It cannot inspect inventory, assign a device, approve
a purchase, schedule a repair, or confirm delivery or return.

When the documents do not establish availability, cost, delivery timing, or
financial responsibility, the assistant should state that the relevant owner
must confirm those details.
