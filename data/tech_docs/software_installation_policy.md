# Software Installation and License Management Policy

Document ID: TECH-004
Department: Technology
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Company: AR Cloud
Document type: Synthetic policy for an educational project

## Purpose and scope

AR Cloud employees use approved software for company work. Technology reviews
technical suitability, installation requirements, security considerations,
and license assignment. Finance manages purchasing and payment controls.

This policy covers desktop applications, browser extensions, software
subscriptions, development tools, and online services used with company
accounts or company information.

An application being free, widely used, or recommended by a colleague does
not automatically make it approved. An existing company subscription does
not mean that every employee has permission to use it or that additional
licenses are available.

Software approval applies to the reviewed use case. Approval for one team
or one type of information does not establish approval for every department
or every category of company data.

## Approved software catalog

Employees should first check the internal approved software catalog. Catalog
entries identify the approved application, supported purpose, installation
method, and any access or license requirements.

Applications available through the managed software portal may be installed
using that portal when the employee meets the catalog requirements. Employees
must not substitute an installer downloaded from an unrelated website.

A catalog entry may require manager approval, application owner approval,
or an assigned license before installation. Availability in the catalog
does not remove those conditions.

If an application is missing from the managed portal or its catalog entry
is unclear, the employee should submit a Technology request. They should
not assume that an older approval or a colleague's installation applies
to their device.

The assistant cannot inspect the current catalog or confirm that a named
application is approved unless the retrieved documents establish that fact.

## Requesting a new application

A new software request should include the application name, official vendor,
business purpose, intended users, required features, and expected duration
of use.

The request should identify the device or environment where the software
will run and describe the categories of company information it will process.
Employees should also explain whether an approved application already
provides the required functionality.

Requests involving a paid license must include an estimated price, currency,
billing frequency, and the manager's approval of the business need. Manager
approval supports the request but does not authorize an employee to purchase
the software independently.

Employees should submit planned requests at least ten working days before
the desired start date. This planning period is not a guaranteed approval,
installation, or procurement deadline.

Urgent requests should explain the business deadline and the impact of
waiting. Urgency does not waive security review, license requirements,
or Finance purchasing controls.

## Technical and security review

Technology reviews compatibility with managed devices, authentication
requirements, administrative privileges, update mechanisms, and available
support options.

For online services, the review also considers the proposed information
handling, access controls, and integration with company accounts. Additional
review by the relevant information owner may be required.

Employees must not upload company information to an unapproved service
while waiting for the review. A trial account does not create an exception
to this rule.

Technology may approve the request, request more information, recommend
an existing alternative, or decline the proposed use. The decision should
state any conditions that apply.

This policy does not promise a universal review duration. Initial support
responses follow TECH-002, while the final review may depend on information
from the requester, vendor, or other responsible owners.

## Purchasing and subscription activation

Technology approval confirms the permitted technical use and its conditions.
Finance purchasing approval confirms whether the proposed purchase may
proceed under the applicable financial process.

Employees must not use a personal payment card or a company card to bypass
the purchasing process. A later reimbursement request does not automatically
make an unauthorized purchase eligible.

Finance confirms the approved purchasing method and any required purchase
reference. Technology coordinates installation or activation after the
required approvals and license arrangements are complete.

An approved purchase request does not prove that a subscription has already
been activated. Employees should wait for the activation instructions or
license assignment confirmation.

This policy does not define spending limits, reimbursement deadlines, or
supplier payment dates. Those questions belong to the applicable Finance
documents.

## Installation and administrative privileges

Employees should use the managed software portal or the installation method
provided by Technology. Installation packages must come from the approved
source specified for the application.

If installation requires administrative privileges, the employee should
request Technology assistance. Employees must not use another person's
administrator credentials or disable device protection to complete setup.

Temporary administrative access, when approved, is limited to the documented
purpose and period. It does not authorize unrelated installations or changes
to company security settings.

Technology may schedule installation when it requires a restart, affects
other applications, or needs employee participation. The employee should
save work and follow the communicated preparation instructions.

An installed application may still require separate account access,
application owner approval, or a license assignment. Installation alone
does not establish permission to use every feature or dataset.

## License assignment and sharing

Technology records license assignments or coordinates them with the
designated application owner. Employees should use the company identity
specified in the activation instructions.

A license assigned to one employee must not be shared through credentials.
Another employee who needs access should submit their own request.

Some products permit shared or concurrent use under specific terms.
Employees must not assume that such terms apply without confirmation
from Technology or the application owner.

If an employee changes roles or no longer needs an application, they should
notify Technology within five working days of that change. Technology
reviews whether the license should be retained, reassigned, or removed.

A license removal does not automatically delete every file created with
the application. The relevant information owner should confirm how work
records will remain accessible.

## Trials, free tools, and browser extensions

Trial software and free tools require approval when they are installed
on company devices or used to process company information.

Employees should disclose trial expiration dates, automatic conversion
to a paid subscription, and any payment details requested during signup.
They must not enter payment information without the required Finance
authorization.

Browser extensions also fall under this policy. Employees should request
review when an extension can read page content, access company applications,
or modify browser behavior.

Approval for a desktop application does not automatically include every
plugin, extension, integration, or optional online feature offered by
the same vendor.

When a trial ends, employees should follow the agreed removal or conversion
plan. A trial expiring does not authorize an independent purchase or justify
moving company information into a personal account.

## Updates and software faults

Employees should allow approved application updates and follow Technology
instructions when a restart or a scheduled maintenance window is required.

They should not downgrade an application, install an unofficial patch,
or disable security controls to work around an error without written
Technology authorization.

A software fault ticket should include the application name, version when
available, device identifier, visible error, approximate time, and effect
on work. Employees should explain whether the issue began after an update.

License activation failures should include the company account identifier
and a description of the activation step that failed. License keys,
passwords, and access tokens must not be pasted into the ticket.

Support priorities and initial response targets follow TECH-002. This
policy does not guarantee that a vendor defect or licensing problem will
be resolved within the initial response period.

## Renewal and cancellation

Employees who own a business subscription should report a known renewal
at least twenty working days before its renewal date. The request should
state whether the software remains necessary and whether user counts
or required features have changed.

Finance and Technology coordinate the relevant purchasing and technical
decisions. Employees must not assume that silence means a renewal has
been approved or that a subscription has been cancelled.

A cancellation request should identify the service, business owner,
renewal date, and any company records that must remain accessible.
The information owner confirms retention requirements before data
is deleted or access is removed.

Submitting a cancellation request does not prove that automatic renewal
has been disabled. Confirmation must come from the responsible owner
or the authorized purchasing process.

This policy does not establish vendor refund rights or a guaranteed
cancellation period for every subscription.

## Department boundaries and assistant limitations

Technology handles software suitability, approved installation, technical
faults, and license access. Finance handles purchasing authorization,
charges, reimbursements, and supplier payments.

HR handles eligibility for professional development benefits. Approval
of a training course under an HR benefit does not automatically approve
a separate software subscription used during that course.

The assistant can explain these procedures and help identify the information
needed for a request. It cannot approve software, purchase a subscription,
assign a license, install an application, or cancel a vendor contract.

When approval status, available licenses, pricing, or renewal status is
not established by the documents, the assistant should direct the employee
to the responsible owner for confirmation.
